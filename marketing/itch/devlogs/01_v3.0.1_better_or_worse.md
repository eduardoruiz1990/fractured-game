# v3.0.1: see at a glance if a token is better

**Type:** Update · **Images:** `loadout_desktop.png`, `loadout_phone.png` (this folder)

---

A small update, and the kind I should have shipped with 3.0.

Until now, choosing a token meant reading two descriptions and doing the maths yourself. Now every token in your inventory carries a badge that compares it with what you are wearing in that slot:

- **▲** better than what you have on
- **▼** worse
- **=** the same
- **≠** it does something different
- **+** that slot is empty, so it's a free gain

Tap or click a token and you get the full comparison: one line per stat, green if you gain, red if you lose. When both tokens change the same stat (two head pieces that both boost your flashlight range, for example), that line is marked **BOTH** and goes first, so you see straight away which one does it better.

Two different tokens only get ▲ or ▼ when every change points the same way. If one makes your dash recharge faster and the other makes your light reach further, the game won't pretend to know which you'd rather have. That one is yours to call.

**Also fixed on PC:** the close button in the menu was cut in half by the window's corner, and the loadout screen left most of its space empty. Your character and the comparison are now on the left and your whole inventory on the right.

If anything in the loadout still feels confusing, tell me in the comments.
