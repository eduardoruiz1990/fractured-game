# Replies for comments

The game page and the devlogs have no comments yet (checked 2026-11-19). These are starting points for the comments that usually come in. Change the details to fit the person, and keep it short: one thank-you, one useful line, one question if you want more.

**Positive comment**
> Thank you for playing, and for taking the time to say so! Which floor did you get to? I'm tuning the later ones right now.

**"Too hard" / died early**
> Thanks for sticking with it! Which floor or boss got you? The desk in the bedroom (between runs) has permanent upgrades you buy with Lucidity, and they carry over, so every run makes the next one easier.

**"Too easy"**
> Good to hear you're cruising! Once you've beaten a boss and reached Patient Level 5, try Intrusive Thoughts in the loadout menu. They make the run harder and pay more Lucidity. And after floor 5 it keeps going: endless mode.

**Bug report**
> Thanks for reporting this! Could you tell me your device (phone model or PC and browser) and what you were doing just before? It'll help me reproduce it.

**Bug fixed (follow-up)**
> This should be fixed in the latest version. A hard refresh will load it. Thanks again for flagging it, let me know if you still see it.

**Performance / lag**
> Sorry about that! Which phone or browser are you on, and roughly when does it slow down (lots of enemies, a boss, from the start)? That tells me where to look.

**Controls question**
> On a phone, drag to move and your light aims itself. Double-tap (or press the DASH button) to dash; a dash makes you briefly untouchable. On PC: WASD to move, the mouse aims, Space dashes, Esc pauses.

**Feature request**
> Love this idea, thanks! I'm writing it down. No promises on timing, but requests like this decide what I build next.

**Non-English comment**
> Reply in their language if you can. A short "thank you" in their language goes a long way.
