# FRACTURED: Survivors is here (v3.0)

FRACTURED has been rebuilt from the ground up. It has a new name, a new core, and almost nothing looks the way it did. If you played the old version: thank you, and please try this one. It is a different game.

## It is a horde game now

The old version sent about one nightmare a second. Now dozens close in at once from every edge of the screen, and everything your flashlight touches burns.

- Rooms keep 10 to 50 enemies alive, growing with every room and floor.
- Kills and pickups climb in pitch while they keep coming, so a good streak sounds like one.
- You level up within seconds of starting, and often after that.
- Sanity no longer drains by itself. What hurts you is what reaches you.

## Your light is your weapon, and it behaves like light

- The beam comes out of the flashlight in your hand.
- Enemies caught in it throw long shadows.
- Walls block it. Enemies behind a wall are in the dark, and safe from the beam, until they come round.
- Outside the beam the dark is dim, not black. You see the eyes first.

## Everything is redrawn and animated

- The character is a jointed figure who walks, looks toward danger, and gets visibly frightened.
- All four Patients look different.
- All six enemy types are redrawn in one style, with legs that actually step.
- Four of the five bosses now walk on jointed legs. The first, Sphere Head, has a new body, plants its feet, braces before it attacks, and watches you.
- Doors are lit arches you walk through. Chests open with rays and a burst.
- Each of the five floors has its own set dressing.

## A new home between runs

The hub is now a bedroom, and it shows your save:

- The **bed** starts a run.
- The **desk** holds your upgrades.
- The **wardrobe** holds your tokens.
- The **shelf** shows a trophy for every boss you have beaten and a jar for each enemy type that fills as you defeat them.
- The **corkboard** pins every Case File page you have found.

## Menus and screens

- Every menu is rebuilt in a dark glass-and-gold style.
- The loadout shows your character with each item beside the body part it dresses.
- The pause screen lists what your run is carrying, and the five achievements you are closest to.
- The Case File has an illustration for each chapter.
- The HUD has an XP bar.

## Easier to start

- One tap to play. No text cards and no tutorial room.
- The first room shows the one control as a picture: drag to move. Your light aims itself.
- Plays in portrait or landscape, on phone or desktop.

## Balance and fixes

- The third boss was a wall that ended almost every run. It has less health, a fairer sweep, and gaps in its final ring of bolts.
- Pausing after clearing a floor could leave you with no way back into the game. Fixed.
- Enemies that steal XP are rarer, and they drop everything they took when you beat them.

## What is the same

The story, the five bosses, twelve weapons, synergies, evolutions, the Daily Session and the endless Recursion are all still there.
