I. EVASION

Move to survive. Press SPACE to Dash. Dashing grants temporary invulnerability against all nightmares. Press ESC or P to pause.

II. DIRECTED THERAPY

Weapons fire automatically. Use your mouse cursor (or right joystick) to aim directional weapons like the Flashlight.

III. THE GRIP (SANITY)

Your health. It drains constantly. Hitting zero triggers a mental breakdown (severe input lag). Drop past zero, and you die.

✧ ✦ ✧
IV. LUCIDITY (XP)

Gather glowing fragments from defeated enemies to manifest new weapons. Collect enough to extract safely.

V. THE VOID

The arena is infinite, but the safe zone is not. Wandering into the dark purple void will rapidly destroy your Grip.

VI. SYNERGIES & TOKENS

Max out weapons (Lv 5) to unlock devastating synergies. Kill Bosses to earn equippable Tokens.

VII. TOKENS

Bosses drop Tokens for Head, Body, Hands, and Legs. Equip up to 4 at once and upgrade their rarity from Common toward Mythic. Matching pieces from the same set unlocks a bonus.

Your permanent rank, raised by how much Lucidity you've spent in SYNAPSE RECORDS. Higher levels unlock deeper upgrades — and eventually, Intrusive Thoughts.

Unlocked at Patient Level 5, after your first Boss kill. Adopt permanent, self-inflicted curses in exchange for Lucidity gain — the riskier the thought, the greater the payout. Stack as many as you can survive.
